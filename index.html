How every figure on the dashboard is calculated from the field records. The calculations follow each survey's analysis sheet.

Phytosociology

Basal area (g)
g = π × DBH² / 40,000 in m², with DBH in cm. π = 3.14 in Surveys 1 and 3 and 3.1416 in Survey 2, as in the sheets.
Density
Absolute DA = n / A (individuals/ha). Relative DR = DA / ΣDA × 100.
Frequency
Absolute FA = plots with the species / total plots. Relative FR = FA / ΣFA × 100. The records in Surveys 1 and 3 do not identify the plot, so each species counts as one occurrence (FA = 1/60), as in the sheet.
Dominance
Absolute DoA = Σg / A (m²/ha). Relative DoR = DoA / ΣDoA × 100.
IVI
IVI = DR + FR + DoR. The values of all species add up to 300.
Sampled area (A)
60 ha in Surveys 1 and 3. In Survey 2, 0.1 ha per plot; the area of each stratum is the number of plots × 0.1 ha (see the Strata tab).

Diversity

Shannon (H′)
H′ = −Σ pᵢ × ln pᵢ, with pᵢ = nᵢ / N. Values below 1.5 indicate low diversity and values between 1.5 and 3.5 moderate diversity.
Simpson (1 − D)
1 − Σ pᵢ²: probability that two individuals picked at random belong to different species (0 to 1).
Pielou (J′)
J′ = H′ / ln S, with S = number of species: how evenly individuals are spread across species (0 to 1).
Survey 2
The indices use the number of records per species in each group (trees, regeneration, grasses, shrubs) and stratum.

Classes and ecological functions

DBH classes
5–10, 10–20, 20–30, 30–40, 40–50 and ≥50 cm. In Survey 4, up to 50–60 and ≥60 cm.
Height classes
0–2, 2–5, 5–10, 10–15 and ≥15 m (Surveys 1 and 3); 0–2, 2–5, 5–10 and ≥10 m (Survey 2 trees); <3, 3–4, 4–5 and ≥5 m (Survey 4). Regeneration, grasses and shrubs use the classes recorded in the field.
Ecological functions
Shade: crown ≥ 5 m. Food: reproductive trees. Shelter and carbon: DBH ≥ 30 cm. Soil: soil stabilisation recorded in the field. N fixation: species of the Fabaceae family.
Outcome of fauna records
Read from each record's observations and remarks: Translocated (translocation completed or successful), Released (release point given), Removed from work area, Kept in place (maintained or retained in situ), Under monitoring (mobilisation not carried out), Not recovered (animal entered a refuge), otherwise Observed.
Retention priority
Classes assigned in the Surveys 1 and 3 analysis sheet, used as they are.
